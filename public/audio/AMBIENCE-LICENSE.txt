winter-fireplace-loop.wav
  Source: "Fireplace #5", sound 2857, Joseph SARDIN.
  https://bigsoundbank.com/fireplace-5-s2857.html
  https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/2857.wav
  Source description: indoor fireplace, realistic recording.
  Equipment: SoundDevices MixPre-3 + Neumann KM184.
  Source format: stereo 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM.
  Used source time 00:02-00:26. No music or added wind layer.
  Strong close-microphone fire pops were smoothly attenuated with a tanh
  transfer curve whose knee is 5 times the excerpt's RMS, before loudness
  normalization. This avoids isolated explosive pops dominating the quiet bed.

Processing common to both
  DC removal; stereo downmix to mono; 2-second equal-power cyclic overlap;
  approximately -22.00 LUFS integrated K-weighted normalization; conversion
  to 16-bit PCM at 48 kHz. Each delivered loop is exactly 22 seconds.
  No encoder delay, added silence, hard clipping or fade-to-silence at the seam.
  The seam uses consecutive samples from the original recording. Its absolute
  sample step is naturally higher for foamy waves than for the fireplace.
  Measurements and SHA-256 checksums are in ambience-measurements.json.

Measured delivered files
  summer: RMS -23.912 dBFS; sample peak -4.203 dBFS; approx -22.000 LUFS.
  winter: RMS -24.628 dBFS; sample peak -4.937 dBFS; approx -22.000 LUFS.
  Each file: 2,112,044 bytes; 1,056,000 samples; mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
  LUFS is a local K-weighted, 400-ms / 100-ms-step gated calculation, not a
  certified mastering measurement. RMS and sample peaks are direct PCM measures.

Playback recommendation
  Decode each WAV once; use native AudioBufferSourceNode loop=true over the full
  buffer. Do not trim the beginning as if it were a short mechanical sample:
  the background starts immediately and the baked cyclic seam must be preserved.
  Only one season source plays. Fade out the old source over 45 ms, stop it,
  then start the latest selected source with a 180 ms fade-in. No crossfade.
  Hidden tabs stop their environmental source. Fireplace playback additionally
  uses a -8 dB high shelf at 1.8 kHz, a 3.8 kHz low-pass (Q 0.55) and gentle
  2:1 compression to soften close crackles.
  Do not pitch-shift either season to create the other. An environment setting
  should control the shared gain of these buses. At -22 LUFS before mixing,
  a gain of 0.25 yields approximately -34 LUFS before the master volume.

Limitations
  These are 22-second excerpts and repeat every 22 seconds. The stereo originals
  were downmixed for a small, neutral background asset. No listening claim or
  specific-device mix approval is implied by the objective measurements; audition
  the final browser mix and check perceived balance on headphones and speakers.
