The MP3 files are the source site's high-quality preview encodings. Leading and
trailing silence are trimmed in memory on decode, with a short tail fade. Shift
is limited to 0.6 seconds, platen to 0.24 seconds and paper to 1.35 seconds. Playback of key
variants has at most +/-1% rate variation and subtle gain variation.

No mechanical oscillator/noise imitation is used. Seasonal environmental
recordings and processing details are documented in AMBIENCE-LICENSE.txt.
